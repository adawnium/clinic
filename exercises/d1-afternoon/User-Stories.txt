User Stories.txt

===

Scenario: a free slot is booked
  Given patient Léa Martin exists
  And nothing is booked on 2026-03-14 between 09:30 and 10:00
  When I book 09:30 for 30 minutes for Léa Martin
  Then the appointment is created
  And it shows in her upcoming appointments

Scenario: an overlapping slot is refused
  Given an appointment on 2026-03-14 from 09:30 to 10:00
  When I book 09:45 for 30 minutes, for any patient
  Then the booking is refused as a conflict
  And the agenda is unchanged

given in the repo
  
  ===

Scenario: a slot is booked on top of another spot :
    Given a patient A exists, and has booked an appointment on time T,
    When another Patient B books an appointment for the same time T,
    Then the appointment of patient B is not created as it makes a conflict.
    The agenda is unchanged.

Scenario : a slot is booked for the past :
    Given a patient A exists, and books an appointment for a time T,
    When the time T is before the current date,
    Then the appointment is not created.
    The agenda remains unchanged.

Scenario : a slot is booked when the clinic is closed :

    Given a patient A exists, and books an appointment for a time T,
    When the time T is not within opening hours of the clinic,
    Then the booking is refused 
    and the agenda is unchanged.

Scenario : someone wants to free up a spot (s)he booked 

    Given Patient A that has booked an appointment on time T,
    When this person cannot attend to said appointment on time T,
    Then the appointment of Patient A is deleted and its time becomes available for another appointment.

Scenario : someone wants to free up a spot (s)he booked but has not booked any spot
    Given Patient A that has not booked an appointment,
    When (s)he wants to free up the spot,
    Then the cancelling is refused, 
    And the agenda remains unchanged.

Scenario : someone wants to reschedule his/her appointment.
    Given Patient A that has booked an appointment on time T,
    When Patient A wants to reschedule his appointment,
    Then an appointment on time T' is created (assuming no one has an appointment on T')
    And appointment on time T is deleted.
    Agenda is updated.
